          ABOUT
          
Three skills,
one pipeline

        

        
Hire a developer, then a designer, then a video guy, and spend six weeks translating between them. Or don't. I run all three, so nothing dies in the handoff.

      


      

        

          
I started writing HTML for a friend's shop in 2018 and never really stopped. Today I build production websites and web apps that hold up under real traffic, and I use generative tooling for the visual layer: campaign posters and short ad films that would otherwise need a studio and a two-lakh budget.

          
The rule I work by: AI is a camera, not a director. Every poster gets composed, colour-graded, and typeset by hand after generation. Every ad film gets a real script and a real edit. That's the gap between output and work you can put a client's logo on.

          
No agency layers, no account manager, no ghosting. You talk to the person building the thing.
